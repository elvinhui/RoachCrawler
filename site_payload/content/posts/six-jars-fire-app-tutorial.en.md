## What is the Six Jars System?

In *Secrets of the Millionaire Mind*, T. Harv Eker suggests dividing your income into six distinct accounts (or "jars"):

1. **Necessities (NEC) - 55%**: For everyday expenses like rent, food, and bills.
2. **Financial Freedom (FFA) - 10%**: **The golden goose!** This money is strictly for buying income-generating assets (stocks, funds). Never spend it!
3. **Long-term Savings (LTSS) - 10%**: For future big purchases like a car, home down payment, or vacations.
4. **Education (EDU) - 10%**: Invest in your greatest asset—your brain. Buy books, courses, or coaching.
5. **Play (PLAY) - 10%**: You MUST spend this every month! Go to a fancy restaurant, get a massage, treat yourself.
6. **Give (GIVE) - 5%**: For charity or gifts to friends and family.
